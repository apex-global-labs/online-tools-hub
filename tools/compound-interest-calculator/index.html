Practical Tips

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is calculated on the principal and on interest accumulated from earlier compounding periods.

Does compounding frequency affect the result?

Yes. With the same positive nominal annual rate and time, more frequent compounding generally produces a higher calculated future value.

Does this include tax, fees or extra deposits?

No. This estimate assumes a fixed rate and principal with no additional deposits, withdrawals, fees or taxes.

What does compounding frequency mean?

It is how often interest is added to the balance. More frequent compounding can change the estimated result when the rate and term are held constant.

Does this calculator predict an investment return?

No. It provides a mathematical estimate based on the values entered and does not account for taxes, fees, changing rates or investment risk.
