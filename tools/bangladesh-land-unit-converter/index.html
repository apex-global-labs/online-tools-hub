Frequently Asked Questions

What land-unit standard does this converter use?

It assumes 1 shotok = 435.6 square feet, 1 katha = 720 square feet and 1 bigha = 20 katha.

Are land units identical throughout Bangladesh?

No. Local conventions can differ by district, locality and deed. Verify the applicable measurement standard before using a result for a transaction.

Does the converter replace a land survey?

No. It is a reference calculator and does not replace official survey, deed review or local land-record guidance.
