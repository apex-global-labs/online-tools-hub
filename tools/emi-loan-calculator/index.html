How to Use EMI / Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount and choose the currency.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and tenure in months.
  3. Select Calculate monthly EMI to view the instalment, total interest and total repayment.

Key Features

  • Calculates fixed-rate monthly reducing-balance EMI.
  • Shows monthly instalment, total interest and total repayment.
  • Handles zero-interest loans and validates tenure and numeric limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment, a fixed monthly payment used to repay a loan over an agreed period under a fixed-rate calculation.

Does the calculator include bank fees or insurance?

No. It excludes processing fees, insurance, taxes, variable-rate changes and lender-specific rounding. Actual offers may differ.

What happens if the interest rate is zero?

The loan amount is divided evenly across the selected number of monthly instalments, with no interest added.
