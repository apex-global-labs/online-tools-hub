How to Use QR Code Generator

  1. Enter plain text or paste a complete website URL.
  2. Choose Default or Custom Mode. In Custom Mode, adjust the colors, dot style and optional background artwork.
  3. Select Generate QR Code to preview the result, then download it as PNG or SVG.

Key Features

  • Creates QR codes from plain text or complete website URLs.
  • Customizes foreground and background colors with square, dots or rounded dot styles.
  • Blends a PNG, JPG or WebP background image with adjustable opacity.
  • Previews the design and downloads PNG or SVG files.
  • Generates the QR code in your browser after the QR library loads.

Practical Tips

Keep strong contrast between the QR pattern and its background. Avoid artwork that makes the pattern hard to distinguish, and scan-test the downloaded PNG or SVG with another device before printing or sharing it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a QR code for a website?

Yes. Paste the complete website address, including https://, or enter plain text.

Can I customize the QR code design?

Yes. Custom Mode lets you change colors and dot style, and blend a PNG, JPG or WebP background image.

Can I add a logo in the centre of the QR code?

This tool currently supports background artwork blending, not a dedicated centre-logo feature.

Can I download the QR code as an SVG?

Yes. You can download the generated QR code as either a PNG or SVG file.

Is my text uploaded to ToolNest?

QR generation happens in your browser after the QR library loads. ToolNest does not receive the text you enter.

Why should I test a QR code before printing it?

A QR code may become difficult to scan if its contrast is weak or artwork covers the pattern. Scan the downloaded file with another device before printing or sharing it.
