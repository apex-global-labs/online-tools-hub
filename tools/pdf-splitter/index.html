PDF Splitter

Split PDF files into separate pages, choose a page range or selected pages, and download individual PDFs or one ZIP archive locally in your browser.

Open PDF Splitter →

How to Use PDF Splitter

  1. Upload one PDF file from your device.
  2. Select individual pages, choose all pages, or enter a range such as 1-3,5.
  3. Choose Split Selected Pages. Download one PDF directly or multiple page files in a ZIP archive.

Key Features

  • Export each selected PDF page as its own PDF document.
  • Choose individual pages or page ranges.
  • Package multiple page PDFs into one ZIP download.
  • Process documents locally in your browser without uploading them to ToolNest.

Practical Tips

Use page ranges to extract only the pages you need. Large documents may be limited by available device memory; this version supports PDFs up to 30 MB and 300 pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save each PDF page separately?

Yes. Select all pages or a page range, split the PDF, and download separate PDF files. Multiple outputs are packaged in one ZIP archive.

Can I split only selected pages?

Yes. Use the page checkboxes or enter a range such as 1-3,5 to choose which pages become separate PDF files.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. PDF processing happens locally in your browser. The PDF and ZIP libraries are loaded from public CDNs when needed; your document is not sent to ToolNest.
