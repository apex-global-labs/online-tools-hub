Bangla & English Text Formatter

Clean Bangla and English text, separate joined Bengali words with a local dictionary, and convert legacy Bijoy ANSI to Unicode or Unicode to Bijoy. This tool processes pasted text only; it does not upload or edit Word documents.

Open Text Formatter →

How to Use Bangla & English Text Formatter

  1. Paste Bangla or English text in Unicode or Bijoy ANSI format.
  2. Choose text cleanup or a supported Bijoy conversion.
  3. Run the formatter, review the result, then copy or download the text.

Key Features

  • Dictionary-backed Bangla joined-word segmentation using a bundled Bengali word list.
  • Two-way Bijoy ANSI ↔ Unicode conversion for pasted text.
  • Bangla and English spacing, punctuation and English camelCase cleanup.
  • Unknown or ambiguous Bangla strings are left unchanged rather than split speculatively.
  • Local browser text processing; text is not uploaded to ToolNest.

Important Tip

Keep a copy of your original text before converting Bijoy and Unicode. Bijoy output may require a compatible SutonnyMJ font.

Bijoy conversion is intended for Bangla legacy ANSI text. English words can be cleaned alongside Bangla text in cleanup mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the tool support both Bangla and English?

Yes. You can paste Bangla and English together for spacing, punctuation and formatting cleanup. Bijoy conversion is intended for Bangla legacy ANSI text.

How does Bangla word separation work?

The tool uses a Bengali word list and conservative dictionary segmentation. Unknown or ambiguous strings are left unchanged rather than split speculatively.

Are my texts uploaded?

No. Text cleanup runs locally in your browser. The Bijoy conversion module loads from a public CDN only when required.
