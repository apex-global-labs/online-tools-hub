How to Use Smart Scanner

  1. Open Camera and grant permission, or choose Add Photos / Images.
  2. Capture or select up to 50 document images. The combined source files must not exceed 200 MB; each image is limited to 25 MB.
  3. Choose Auto Color / Clean, Grayscale or Black & White enhancement and set the PDF page size.
  4. Review the automatically refreshed PDF preview. Reorder or remove pages if needed.
  5. Download one combined PDF or separate one-page PDFs together as a ZIP.

Key Features

  • Rear-camera capture on supported mobile browsers and image upload from phone or computer.
  • Automatic document-edge detection and perspective correction when a clear quadrilateral boundary is found.
  • Color cleanup, grayscale and black-and-white document enhancement.
  • Multi-page scanning with page reordering and removal.
  • One combined PDF or one PDF per page packaged in a ZIP archive.
  • Permanent small ToolNest branding at the bottom of every PDF page.
  • Local browser processing; selected photos are not uploaded to ToolNest.

Practical Tips

Place the document on a contrasting, flat surface and keep all four edges visible. Edge detection may not work for every background, shadow or low-resolution photo, so inspect the preview before using an important document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I scan documents with my phone camera?

Yes. Open Camera, allow camera access and capture each page. Camera availability depends on browser permissions and HTTPS.

How many images can I scan at once?

Up to 50 images can be added in one job, with a combined source-file limit of 200 MB and a 25 MB per-image limit.

Does the scanner straighten tilted documents?

It attempts automatic edge detection and perspective correction when a clear document boundary is visible. Review the preview because difficult backgrounds or low contrast can prevent reliable detection.

Can I download separate PDFs together?

Yes. Download all pages as one PDF or create one PDF per page and download them together in a ZIP archive.

Can I remove ToolNest branding from the PDF?

No. A small ToolNest footer is permanently included on every generated PDF page.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photo processing and PDF generation happen locally in your browser. Public CDN libraries are loaded when needed, but selected image files are not sent to ToolNest.
