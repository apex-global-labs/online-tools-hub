Find & Replace

Find text and replace one or all matches with optional case and whole-word matching.

Open Find & Replace

How to Use Find & Replace

  1. Paste the text you want to edit.
  2. Enter the phrase to find and its replacement.
  3. Choose matching options and select Find & Replace.

Key Features

  • Replace the first match or all matches.
  • Optional case-sensitive matching.
  • Optional whole-word matching with Unicode-aware boundaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool change my original text?

The original input remains in its box; the changed text appears separately in the result.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Find and replace runs locally in your browser.

Can I replace only the first match?

Yes. Turn off Replace all matches to replace only the first occurrence.

Helpful Tips and Limitations
