URL Shortener

Turn a long website address into a shorter link that is easier to share in messages, posts and documents. You can also request an optional custom alias when it is available.

Open URL Shortener →

How to Use URL Shortener

  1. Open the ToolNest URL Shortener.
  2. Paste a complete website address beginning with https:// or http://.
  3. Optionally enter a custom alias containing 3–16 letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores.
  4. Select Shorten URL, then copy the short link after it is created.

Important: The short link is hosted by an independent shortening provider, not on the ToolNest domain. The submitted destination URL is sent to that provider to create and maintain the redirect.

Key features

  • Shortens valid HTTP and HTTPS website addresses.
  • Optional custom alias, subject to provider availability and rules.
  • Copy-ready short link and a direct link to open the result.
  • No ToolNest account, paid subscription or ToolNest backend required.
  • Shortening requests are subject to the provider's anonymous rate limits and service availability.

Privacy and link safety

ToolNest does not save the URLs entered into this tool. When you create a short link, the URL is sent directly from your browser to the external provider. That provider may store the destination and the generated short link under its own privacy policy and terms.

Short links can hide the destination address. Only shorten links you trust, and tell recipients what the link is for. Check a short link's destination before opening unfamiliar links. ToolNest cannot guarantee the availability or safety of third-party redirects.

Frequently asked questions

How do I shorten a long URL?

Paste a complete HTTP or HTTPS URL, optionally enter a custom alias, and select Shorten URL. Copy the generated short link to share it.

Are ToolNest short links hosted on the ToolNest domain?

No. The free integration creates links on the third-party provider's domain. ToolNest does not currently provide its own redirect domain or link-management dashboard.

Is the URL shortening service free?

The integrated provider currently offers anonymous free use (20 requests per minute and 1,000 per day, according to its published API v1 limits). Availability, limits and provider terms may change.

Does ToolNest store the URLs I shorten?

ToolNest does not store the submitted URL. It is sent directly from your browser to the shortening provider, which processes and stores the short link under its own policies.
