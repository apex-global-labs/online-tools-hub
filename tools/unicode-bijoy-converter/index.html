Unicode ⇄ Bijoy Converter

Convert Bangla text between standard Unicode and legacy Bijoy ANSI using two side-by-side editors. Choose the direction you need, review the converted text, then copy or download it.

How to Use Unicode ⇄ Bijoy Converter

  1. Paste Unicode Bangla into the left editor or Bijoy ANSI text into the right editor.
  2. Choose Unicode → Bijoy or Bijoy → Unicode.
  3. Review the converted text, then copy or download the required format.

Key Features

  • Two side-by-side editors.
  • Independent conversion controls for both directions.
  • Copy, clear, swap and TXT download controls.
  • Browser-based processing; text is not uploaded to ToolNest.

Important Note

Bijoy ANSI text may look like Latin characters unless a compatible SutonnyMJ font is installed and selected. Unicode is recommended for websites, social media and mobile devices. Keep a copy of the original text and review names and conjuncts after conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Bijoy text look like English characters?

Bijoy ANSI stores legacy character codes. To display the Bangla glyphs correctly, use a compatible SutonnyMJ font in an application that supports it.

Which side should I paste my text into?

Paste standard Bangla Unicode into the Unicode editor and legacy Bijoy ANSI text into the Bijoy editor. Then select the conversion button for the direction you need.

Is my text uploaded to ToolNest?

No. The conversion runs in your browser. The conversion module is loaded from a public CDN when first needed.
