Text Sorter

Sort lines alphabetically or numerically while controlling blank lines and letter case.

Open Text Sorter

How to Use Text Sorter

  1. Paste or type one item per line.
  2. Choose alphabetical or numeric sorting and your blank-line preference.
  3. Select Sort lines, then copy or download the result.

Key Features

  • Alphabetical ascending and descending order.
  • Numeric sorting for values such as 2, 10 and 100.
  • Optional case-insensitive comparison and blank-line preservation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is sorting done online?

Sorting runs locally in your browser; your text is not sent to a server.

Does numeric sorting handle 2 and 10 correctly?

Yes. It uses numeric-aware locale comparison.

Can I sort numbers as numbers instead of text?

Yes. Choose Numeric ascending or Numeric descending to sort values such as 2 and 10 in numeric order.

When should I use numeric sorting instead of alphabetical sorting?

Use numeric sorting when lines represent numbers and you want numerical order, such as 2 before 10. Alphabetical sorting compares text characters.

Can I preserve blank lines while sorting?

The tool includes a blank-line option. Choose the behavior that fits your text and check the output for section boundaries.

Helpful Tips and Limitations

Place one item on each line for predictable results.
