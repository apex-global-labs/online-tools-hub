Text TOOL

Text Sorter

Sort lines alphabetically or numerically with optional case-insensitive matching.

Open Text Sorter

How to use

Paste one item per line, choose A–Z, Z–A or numeric order, configure blank-line handling and sort locally.

Important notes

Numeric-aware sorting handles values such as 2 and 10 in their expected numeric order.
