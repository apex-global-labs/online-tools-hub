Word & Character Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs. Use this free browser-based word & character counter to work with your content quickly without creating an account.

How to Use Word & Character Counter

  1. Paste or type text into the input area.
  2. Choose Count text to calculate the statistics.
  3. Review the counts and copy the summary if needed.

Key Features

  • Counts words and characters.
  • Also reports sentence and paragraph counts where available.
  • Processes text locally in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool count words?

Words are identified by separating the text at whitespace boundaries.

Are spaces included in the character count?

Spaces and line breaks are included in the text length counted by this tool.

Can I count a long document?

You can paste text into the input; very large documents may be limited by your browser and device memory.
