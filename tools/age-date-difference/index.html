How to Use Age & Date Difference

  1. Choose the starting date.
  2. Choose an ending date, or keep the default current date.
  3. Select Calculate and review the date difference.

Key Features

  • Shows years, months and days between dates.
  • Also reports the total number of days.
  • Checks for invalid or reversed date ranges.

Practical Tips

Check the date format and the intended time zone when comparing records created near midnight or across regions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age?

Yes. Enter your birth date as the starting date and use today's date as the ending date.

Can the ending date be earlier than the starting date?

No. The ending date must be the same as or later than the starting date.

Are my dates stored?

No. The date calculation is performed in your browser.

Does the age calculator account for leap years?

The result is calculated from the selected calendar dates, so the number of days can vary around leap years and month lengths.

Which date should I enter to calculate age?

Enter the person's date of birth as the start date and the date you want to calculate age on as the end date.
