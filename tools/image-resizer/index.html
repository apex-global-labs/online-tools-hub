Image Resizer

Resize a local PNG, JPEG or WebP image to custom pixel dimensions while optionally keeping its aspect ratio. Images are processed in your browser.

Set image dimensions in pixels

Open Image Resizer →

How to Use Image Resizer

  1. Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image under 10 MB.
  2. Enter the target width and height in pixels and choose whether to keep the aspect ratio.
  3. Select Resize image, preview the result and download it.

Key Features

  • Processes images locally in the browser.
  • Supports PNG, JPEG and WebP output.
  • Limits target dimensions to 4096 pixels per side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image resizing uses your browser's image and canvas features.

What is aspect ratio?

Aspect ratio is the relationship between an image's width and height. Keeping it prevents the image from appearing stretched.

What image sizes are supported?

This version accepts PNG, JPEG and WebP files up to 10 MB and limits output dimensions to 4096 by 4096 pixels.
