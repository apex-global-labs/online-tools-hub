How to Use Forex Currency Converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Choose the source and target currencies.
  3. Select Convert currency and review the converted amount and rate update time.

Key Features

  • Supports many currency pairs provided by the rate service.
  • Shows the reference exchange rate and its update timestamp.
  • Fetches rates when you request a conversion.

Practical Tips

Treat displayed rates as reference values. Banks and payment providers may apply different rates, spreads or fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these live trading prices?

No. The tool displays reference exchange rates from its rate provider; they may differ from trading or payment prices.

Why might a currency conversion fail?

The rate service may be unavailable, the pair may not be supported, or your connection may be offline.

Are bank fees included?

No. Bank, card, transfer and exchange-provider fees are not included.

Are the displayed exchange rates live trading prices?

No. The converter shows reference rates retrieved from its rate provider. They may differ from prices offered by banks, exchange services or trading platforms.

Why can the converted amount differ from what a bank pays?

Banks and payment providers may apply spreads, service fees and their own rate updates. Check the provider's final quote before making a transaction.
