Advanced PDF Merger

Merge PDF files and select, reorder or rotate individual pages before downloading one custom PDF.

Merge PDFs with page-level control

Open Advanced PDF Merger →

How to Use Advanced PDF Merger

  1. Add two or more PDF files, or add one PDF if you want to extract selected pages.
  2. Reorder files and pages, untick pages you do not need, or apply a page range such as 1-3,5.
  3. Choose Merge Selected Pages, then download your new PDF.

Key Features

  • Combine multiple PDF documents in a chosen file and page order.
  • Select or exclude individual pages and apply page ranges to each PDF.
  • Move files and pages, rotate selected pages, and create a new PDF locally in your browser.

Practical Tips

Check the page selection and order before merging. Keep a copy of your original PDFs, especially when working with important documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I merge only selected pages from PDF files?

Yes. Add your PDFs, select the pages you want to keep, and merge the selected pages into one new document. You can also use a page range such as 1-3,5.

Can I change the order or rotation of pages?

Yes. Move files or individual pages up and down, and rotate individual pages before creating the merged PDF.

Are my PDF files uploaded to ToolNest?

No. The tool processes PDF files in your browser and does not upload them to the ToolNest server. Very large files may be limited by your device memory.
