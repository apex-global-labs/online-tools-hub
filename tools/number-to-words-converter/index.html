Number to Words Converter

Convert numeric values into written words in English or Bangla. Supports negative numbers and up to two decimal places, with browser-local processing and no external API.

How to Use Number to Words Converter

  1. Enter a whole number or a value with up to two decimal places.
  2. Choose English or বাংলা as the output language.
  3. Select Convert to words, then copy or download the written result.

Key Features

  • Supports English and Bangla number words.
  • Handles negative values and up to two decimal places.
  • Processes values locally in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which number range is supported?

The converter supports values from negative 999,999,999,999 to positive 999,999,999,999.

Can I convert decimal numbers?

Yes. Up to two decimal places are supported and read digit by digit after the decimal point.

Does it support Bangla output?

Yes. Select বাংলা to receive the number words in Bangla.
